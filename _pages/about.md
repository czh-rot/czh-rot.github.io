<p align="justify">
  I am <strong>Zehao Chen</strong>, a Ph.D. student at the 
  <a href="https://www.sdu.edu.cn/">School of Cyber Science and Technology</a>, Shandong University.
  My supervisor is Professor <a href="https://faculty.sdu.edu.cn/ju_lei/zh_CN/index.htm">Lei Ju</a>.
</p>

<p align="justify">
  <strong>Research Interests:</strong>
</p>
<ul>
  <li>Private Information Retrieval (PIR), Fully Homomorphic Encryption (FHE)</li>
  <li>Computational / In-Storage / Near-Data Computing</li>
  <li>Key-Value Stores (e.g., LSM-tree, blockchain storage)</li>
  <li>Computational Storage Devices (CSDs), ReRAM-based Processing-in-Memory (PIM)</li>
</ul>
